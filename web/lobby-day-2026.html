Summer Conference & Lobby Day 2026

Introduction

Every summer, CCL volunteers from across the country gather in Washington, D.C. for two days of training and connection, followed by a day of lobbying on Capitol Hill. This year's conference, The Path to Clean Energy Runs Through D.C., was held July 26–28, 2026 at the Omni Shoreham Hotel, with Lobby Day meetings taking place on Capitol Hill on July 28.

SVE members joined the rest of the California delegation for training sessions, a group photo on the steps of the Library of Congress, and meetings with congressional offices — including Rep. Ro Khanna's office (CA-17) and Rep. Zoe Lofgren's office — to press for federal action on clean, affordable energy. Members also took some time to enjoy the U.S. Botanic Garden between sessions.
